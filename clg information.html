Admission Procedure

  1. Check the eligibility criteria for your chosen course.
  2. Complete the online or offline application form.
  3. Submit the required certificates and documents.
  4. Attend the entrance examination or counselling, if applicable.
  5. Complete fee payment and document verification.
  6. Confirm your admission and join the college.

Note: Actual admission requirements depend on the course and institution.
